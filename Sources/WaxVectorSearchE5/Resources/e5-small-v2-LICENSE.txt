e5-small-v2.mlmodelc is a CoreML conversion of intfloat/e5-small-v2
(https://huggingface.co/intfloat/e5-small-v2, revision ffb93f3bd4047442299a41ebb6fa998a38507c52),
from "Text Embeddings by Weakly-Supervised Contrastive Pre-training" (Wang et al., 2022,
arXiv:2212.03533), part of microsoft/unilm. Wax converted it with scripts/convert_e5_small_v2.py:
masked mean pooling and L2 normalization added to the graph, weights quantized to INT8.
